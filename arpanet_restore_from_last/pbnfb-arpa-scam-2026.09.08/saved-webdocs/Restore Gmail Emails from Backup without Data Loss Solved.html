DIY Solutions to Restore Gmail Account from Backup without Data Loss

Method 1: Recover from Trash or Bin (within 30 days)

  1. Login to Gmail Account.
  2. Click on trash or bin option.
  3. Enable emails to restore.
  4. Click move to Inbox or choose desired folder.

Note: Gmail will automatically permanently delete trash or bin folder emails after 30 days. Ensure to restore Gmail emails from backup.

Method 2: Restore Via Google Takeout Archive Using Betterbird

  1. Download Betterbird email client on your PC.
  2. Next, configure your Gmail account in Betterbird using Add Account option.
  3. Now, search for ImportExportNG Tools and click on Add button.
  4. After that, go to the Local Folder and right-click and choose ImportExportNG Tools.
  5. Thereafter, choose import MBOX file and select Gmail data to open.
  6. Finally, drag and drop the inserted Gmail backup data to configured Gmail account.

Risk of Data Duplication: There is always a risk of data corruption while using DIY approach to restore Gmail account. Your critical Gmail backup data might get corrupted during the import process.
