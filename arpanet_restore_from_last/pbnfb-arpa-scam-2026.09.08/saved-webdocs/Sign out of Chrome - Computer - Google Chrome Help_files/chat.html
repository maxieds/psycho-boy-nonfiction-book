Sign out of Chrome - Computer

To sign out of your Google Account from Chrome, you can follow these simple steps on your computer.

How to sign out of Chrome

  1. On your computer, open Chrome.
  2. At the top right, select Profile and then Sign out of Chrome.

Official Google help source

Tip: If you signed out of a Google Web Service like Gmail or your Google Account is in an error state, select Remove Account from Chrome.

When you sign out of a Google Web Service like Gmail, to continue to save new data to your Google Account, you’ll need to verify it’s you.

For technical details and specific data practices, you can refer to the full article: Sign out of Chrome
